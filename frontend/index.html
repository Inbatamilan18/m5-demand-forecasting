Walmart M5 Retail Demand Forecasting

This system forecasts daily unit sales for every product in every Walmart store, 28 days into the future. It is built on the M5 competition dataset, which covers 5 years of real Walmart sales across three US states.

Retailers must balance stockouts against overstocking. Forecasts are produced at the lowest level — item × store — then aggregated upward through all 12 hierarchy levels, so store managers, category planners and executives all read consistent numbers.
